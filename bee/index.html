Bee

Make honey

Bees are crazy, they make so much honey, and that stuff is so good. Bees live in large colonies called beehives. If I was a bee, I would be one of the drone bees.

Life cycle

  1. Eggs are laid in the wax honeycomb and the queen chooses to make them a boy or a girl
  2. Worker bees clean the hive and feed the larvae
  3. Bees do a little dance called the waggle dance to tell other bees where the honey is
  4. When bees get the honey they do a tremble dance to tell other bees to get the honey off them
  5. New queen bees go out and get busy with multiple drones, all of whom die after mating
  6. The mated queen forms a new colony
